# 🛡️ XHere Report & Appeal System Guide

Welcome to the XHere Report & Appeal System! This guide will help you understand how to:
- Report problematic locations
- Submit evidence
- Appeal moderation decisions
- Track transparency and community health

---

## 🚩 1. Reporting a Location

If you find a location that violates community guidelines, is spam, fake, or otherwise problematic, you can report it!

### **How to Report:**
1. **Find the location** you want to report.
2. Click the **📝 Report** button (usually on the location card or details page).
3. In the **Report Location** modal:
   - **Select a report type:**
     - 🚫 Spam
     - ⚠️ Inappropriate
     - 🔄 Duplicate
     - ❌ Fake
     - 😡 Offensive
     - 📝 Other
   - **Describe the issue** in the reason box.
   - **(Optional) Add evidence:**
     - 📎 Upload files (screenshots, photos, etc.)
     - 📝 Add text evidence (details, links, etc.)
   - **(Optional) Submit anonymously** (you can provide an email for follow-up if you wish).
4. Click **📤 Submit Report**.
5. You’ll see a confirmation and the report will be sent to moderators for review.

---

## 📎 2. Submitting Evidence

- You can attach multiple files or text notes as evidence.
- Evidence helps moderators make fair decisions.
- All evidence is kept confidential and only visible to moderators.

---

## ⚖️ 3. The Appeal Process

If your location is removed or flagged and you believe it was a mistake, you can appeal!

### **How to Appeal:**
1. Go to your **profile** or the notification about your removed location.
2. Click **Appeal** next to the affected location.
3. In the **Appeal Modal**:
   - **Explain why the location should be restored.**
   - **Attach any supporting evidence** (photos, documents, etc.).
   - **Provide a contact email** for updates (optional).
4. Click **Submit Appeal**.
5. Moderators will review your appeal and notify you of the outcome.

## 📝 5. Tips & Best Practices

- **Be specific:** The more details and evidence you provide, the faster and fairer the review.
- **Stay respectful:** Use the system for genuine issues, not personal disputes.
- **Check the dashboard:** See how XHere is keeping the community safe and fair!
- **Appeal if needed:** Everyone deserves a second look—don’t hesitate to appeal if you think a mistake was made.

## ❓ FAQ

**Q: Can I report anonymously?**
- Yes! Just check the anonymous option. You can provide an email if you want updates.

**Q: What happens after I report?**
- Moderators review your report, may request more info, and take action if needed. You’ll be notified of the outcome.

**Q: How long do appeals take?**
- Most appeals are reviewed within a few days. Check the Transparency Dashboard for average times.

**Q: Is my evidence private?**
- Yes, only moderators can see your evidence.
